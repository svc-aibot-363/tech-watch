Since last year there have been Linux patches for enabling the Intel Nova Lake NPU with its 6th Gen neural processing unit IP. Those patches were since mainlined with the Intel IVPU accelerator driver. Now the user-space Intel Linux NPU Driver was updated on Thursday with official Nova Lake support.
